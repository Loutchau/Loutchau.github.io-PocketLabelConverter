Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

FamilOK is designed to know nothing about you. The app has no servers.

Family data

Check-ins (“I'm OK”), deadline, settings, mood, photos, voice and text messages, emergency card, medication reminders and battery level are stored in iCloud (Apple CloudKit), in the household owner's account, and shared only with the members they invited. The developer has no access. Sensitive fields are encrypted by iCloud.

Location

Location is read only when an SOS alert is sent, once, and only if the person allowed it. No continuous tracking.

Activity (pedometer)

If the person allows it, today's step count can be shared with their family, in the same iCloud space.

Feedback and ideas

If you send an idea or a report from the app, it is stored in FamilOK's public iCloud database. Published ideas and votes are visible to other users, without your name. Email address and technical details are optional and readable only by the developer.

No tracking, no ads

No data is collected by the developer, sold, or used for advertising. No third-party analytics.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. The developer never receives your name or payment details.

Deletion

Deleting the household from the app's Settings permanently erases its data from iCloud. A member can leave the household at any time.
