Not an emergency service

FamilOK helps families stay in touch. It is not an emergency service, a medical device or a medical alert system. Alerts depend on the iPhone, the network, iCloud and notification settings; they may be delayed or not arrive. In danger, call emergency services.

FamilOK Household+ subscription

Household+ is a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription, with a one-month free trial for new subscribers. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation (or at the end of the trial). It renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period, from Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Basic safety (1 person cared for, 2 family members, alerts, SOS) stays free.
