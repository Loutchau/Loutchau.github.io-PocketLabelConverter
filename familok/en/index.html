FamilOK

One tap a day to reassure the whole family. Your parent taps “I'm OK”; if they forget, their family is alerted.

Support

A question, a problem, an idea? In the app: Settings › An idea? A problem?. Every message is read.

FAQ

My parent didn't get the invitation. Send the link again from “My family”, or show them the QR code. They must be signed in to iCloud on their iPhone (Settings › their name).

I don't get alerts. Make sure FamilOK notifications are allowed (Settings › Notifications › FamilOK) and Background App Refresh is on. The alert is prepared on your own phone: open FamilOK now and then to keep it up to date.

Where is my data? In your family's iCloud, nowhere else. See Privacy.

How do I cancel? iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › FamilOK. Basic safety stays free.

FamilOK is not an emergency service and does not replace a medical alert system. In danger, call emergency services.
