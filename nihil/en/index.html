Nihil

A minimalist idle game. Points of light fall through the dark: tap them, grow your universe, then begin again, stronger, with every Big Bang.

Support

A question, a bug, an idea? Write to support@loutchau.dev. Every message is read.

Frequently asked

My purchases are missing. Open the Shop (✦ button) and tap “Restore purchases”. The Starter pack and the Supporter pack come back on any device using the same Apple Account.

Do I need an internet connection? No. Game Center leaderboards update the next time you are online.

Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.

How do I change the language? In the game: ⋯ button › Your universe › Language.

Does “Reset progress” remove my purchases? No: Fragments, the Supporter pack and the Eclipse universe are kept.
