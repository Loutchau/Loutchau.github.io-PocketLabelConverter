Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Nihil collects nothing. The game has no servers, no account, no analytics and no tracking. The developer receives no data about you.

Your game

Your progress and settings are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

Optional. If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores (lifetime light, best combo) are sent to Apple to show leaderboards, under Apple's privacy policy.

Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. The developer never receives your name or payment details; the game only learns which pack was bought, to deliver it.

No ads, no tracking

No data is sold, shared or used for advertising. No third-party analytics.

Children

Nihil is suitable for everyone and collects no personal data from anyone, children included.
