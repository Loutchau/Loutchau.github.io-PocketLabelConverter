Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

VolumeBar collects nothing. The app has no servers, no account, no analytics and no tracking, and makes no network connections. The developer receives no data about you.

Your apps' sound

To set each app's volume, VolumeBar uses the macOS “System Audio Recording” permission. The app's sound passes through VolumeBar and goes straight back out to your audio output at the volume you chose. It is never recorded, kept or sent, and an app left at 100% is not touched.

Your settings

The volume you choose for each app is saved on your Mac only. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchase

Payment is handled entirely by Apple. The developer never receives your name or payment details.

No ads, no tracking

No data is sold, shared or used for advertising. No third-party analytics.

Children

VolumeBar is suitable for everyone and collects no personal data from anyone, children included.
