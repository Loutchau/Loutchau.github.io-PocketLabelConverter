VolumeBar

Each app's volume, right from the Mac menu bar. Turn the music down without touching your call, mute a noisy tab without muting everything else.

Frequently asked

Which macOS version do I need? macOS 14.2 or later.

An app is missing from the list. An app shows up as soon as it plays sound. Play some music or a video in that app, then open VolumeBar again.

Why does macOS ask for “System Audio Recording” permission? It is the only way on a Mac to change the sound of one app in particular: VolumeBar routes that app's sound through itself to change its volume in real time. Nothing is recorded, kept or sent.

The slider doesn't change anything. Make sure VolumeBar is allowed in System Settings › Privacy & Security, under system audio recording, then quit and reopen VolumeBar.

Are my settings remembered? Yes: the volume you choose for each app is kept on this Mac and applied again when the app plays sound.

Do I need an internet connection? No. VolumeBar works entirely offline.

How do I quit VolumeBar? Click its icon in the menu bar, then “Quit VolumeBar”.
